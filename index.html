Questions

Good questions,
honest answers.

Is MindDock only for people with an ADHD diagnosis?
No. MindDock is designed around the cognitive challenges that come with ADHD — time blindness, executive dysfunction, overwhelm — but it works for anyone who struggles with traditional task managers. A diagnosis is not required.
How does the capacity bucket actually work?
When you set your energy level (1–5), MindDock runs a scoring algorithm: Score = (Priority × 1.5) + Urgency + EnergyMatch. It then greedily packs tasks into a time budget for that energy level — e.g. Level 3 (Steady) gets 105 minutes. Tasks that don't fit are hidden, not deleted. They come back when you have more capacity or when energy increases.
What is the Time-Blindness Multiplier and can I reset it?
Every time you finish a Focus session, MindDock compares your estimate to real working time and updates your multiplier using an exponentially weighted moving average: B_new = B_old × 0.7 + (actual ÷ estimated) × 0.3. It starts at 1.5× and adjusts over time. You can reset it from the Settings panel — or reset all app data to start fresh.
Is my data private? Who can see it?
Your data is stored in Supabase with Row-Level Security — meaning the database enforces that only your authenticated account can read or write your rows. We never sell your data, never use it to train AI models, and never share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers listed in the Privacy Policy.
Does it work offline?
Yes. MindDock is local-first — all data is persisted to localStorage so the app works fully without a network connection. When you reconnect, changes sync to the cloud. A full sync also runs automatically before you sign out to prevent any data loss.
Is there a mobile app?
MindDock is a Progressive Web App — open it in Chrome or Safari on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen" to get a native-feeling app icon. A dedicated Android app is on the roadmap.
What does "scaffolded task" mean?
Scaffolded tasks are large recurring chores broken into their smallest individual steps — "Start a load of laundry", "Move clothes to dryer", "Fold and put away". During onboarding you pick which life areas pile up for you and MindDock creates your first set of scaffolds automatically. They're designed so that starting feels smaller because each step is genuinely tiny.
